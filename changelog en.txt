3.0.1
## Additions
Tool Menu: a "Send report to Discord" button next to Attach to Game, for when the editor can't attach
## Tools
Updated to 7.2.1 and out of maintenance: Match Time, Player Level, Special Moves, Unlimited Hero/Fabled in Team, Free Buy Shop, Free Buy Spirit Market, Add Player Spirits and End Match 99-0 (Fallback)
## Redesigns
New animated look for Custom Passives (Hero / Fabled and Summon Players), Player Spirits and Add Spirits
Report Unknown Ability now opens inside the editor, with a new animated design
Dropdown menus now unfold with an animation, their items slide in and the selected one is highlighted
## Improvements
Custom Passives: Hero / Fabled now only lists hero passives and Summon Players only lists normal passives
Give XP Orbs / XP to Crates: Locate no longer freezes the editor, a loading screen shows while it searches
Special Moves: once the Abilearn Board is captured, the slots without a move show "-----"
Special Moves now lights up in the Tool Menu while editing is on, and the Custom Passives menu shows which hook (Summon / Hero-Fabled) is active
## Bug Fixes
Fixed the editor sometimes not attaching to the game even though it was open
Fixed Special Moves keeping the previous player's moves in the slots the new player has empty
Fixed Locate (Give XP Orbs / XP to Crates) sometimes failing the first time and working on the second try
Fixed Give XP Orbs sometimes setting your current quantity instead of the one you typed
