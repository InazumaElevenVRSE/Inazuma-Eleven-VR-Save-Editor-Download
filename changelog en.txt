3.0.2
## Additions
Player Spirits: a Spirit List with every spirit (over 5,600) and the player's picture, click one to put its ID in the Spirit Player ID field
Reports: your Discord picture now shows in the Reports tab and in the reports you send

## Tools
Fut Draft is enabled again

## Redesigns
New animated developer card
Add to Steam: a new animated window that shows the library preview, each step live (closing Steam, writing the shortcut, reopening Steam) and the result

## Improvements
The editor now scales down to fit smaller screens (e.g. 1366x768) so no options get cut off, and adapts when moved to another monitor
The game folder is found in more Steam setups (custom libraries, renamed folders, per-user installs), so the EAC launcher swap works for more people
Clearer logs when the EAC launcher can't be swapped or the editor can't attach, to help with reports
Tool Menu: "Send report to Discord" now asks you to connect your Discord account first, like the Reports tab

## Bug Fixes
The menus that open inside the editor no longer let you click what's behind them
Fixed Player Spirits always adding the default spirit (BCE81249) instead of the ID you typed
Fixed Increment Spirits always adding 1 instead of the amount you picked (1-99)
Fixed values typed in a box not being applied when you clicked a button or switch right after
